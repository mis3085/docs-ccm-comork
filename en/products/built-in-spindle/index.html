Advanced Spindle Technology for High-Speed Machining
#

The built-in spindle represents the forefront of high-speed machining solutions, delivering high variable speed, precision, and efficiency for demanding manufacturing environments. Engineered for exceptional performance, these spindles excel in minimizing noise and vibration, contributing to reduced cycle times and improved overall productivity.

During production, comprehensive spectrum measurements are conducted to guarantee optimal running characteristics and assembly quality, ensuring the highest rotational accuracy. The modular design philosophy underpins product stability, extended service life, and cost-effective maintenance.

Key Features
#

  • High rotational speed and precision
  • Low noise and vibration for improved machining quality
  • Modular construction for enhanced stability and longevity
  • Rigorous spectrum measurement during production for quality assurance
  • Optimized for mould and high-quality processing applications
